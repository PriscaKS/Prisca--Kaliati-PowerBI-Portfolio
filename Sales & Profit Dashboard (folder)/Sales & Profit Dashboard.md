## Business Problem

Organizations generate large volumes of sales data every day, making it challenging to identify profitable products, understand customer purchasing behaviour, and monitor overall business performance.

This dashboard converts transactional data into interactive reports that simplify analysis and support informed business decisions.

---

## Objectives

- Analyse sales performance
- Monitor profitability
- Identify high-performing products
- Compare regional sales
- Evaluate customer purchasing trends
- Track key business KPIs
